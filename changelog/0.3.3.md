# Inventors 0.3.3 — Stone/Copper Progression Completion

## Copper is now a real age
- Rebuilt the Copper chapter around Firstworks advanced ceramics, Heavy Leather, Bellows, Crucible Furnace, primitive casting, annealing, hammer-working, wire, fasteners, dedicated copper tools/utilities, textile upgrades, Rotary Quern, lime, and plaster.
- The Copper capstone now proves the complete primitive workshop instead of accepting Productive Metalworks or generic Silent Gear as the age proof.
- Stone completion is enforced at the Copper boundary with Hafting Compound, closing the renewable-resin/strong-binding loop.

## Productive Metalworks moved to Bronze
- Productive Metalworks Fire Clay, Black Fire Bricks, Black Foundry Controller, and Ingot Cast now begin after the Copper capstone and Tin boundary.
- Fire Clay is re-authored from Firstworks refractory clay + plaster.
- The Black Foundry Controller is re-authored to require Firstworks Copper Fasteners and a fired Crucible, physically preventing a mature-foundry bootstrap around primitive Copper.
- Existing furnace/smoker/blast-furnace Tin and Copper ingot shortcuts remain removed, so early Tin ore cannot advance progression before the Bronze foundry.

## Cleanup
- Removed the old KubeJS Copper recipes that turned copper ingots directly into vanilla shears/buckets; Firstworks Copper Shears and Copper Buckets are now the intended utilities.
- Preserved the existing Copper and Bronze capstone quest IDs so later chapters keep their dependency graph intact.
