# Inventors

A Minecraft modpack centered around **Create** and **Clockwork** mods, designed to unleash your creativity and engineering prowess.

## About This Pack

Inventors is a modpack that empowers players to design, build, and innovate. Whether you want to construct intricate mechanical contraptions or build your own vehicles to explore the world, this pack provides all the tools you need.

## Key Features

### Create Mod
Build complex mechanical systems with:
- Kinetic energy networks powered by water wheels, windmills, and steam engines
- Automated processing chains for mining, farming, and manufacturing
- Moving contraptions using chassis, pistons, and rope pulleys
- Logic gates and redstone integration for smart automation

### Clockwork Mod
Take your creations to the skies with:
- Airship and vehicle construction
- Custom hull designs and modular components
- Navigation systems for exploration
- Integration with Create's mechanical systems

### Additional Mods
- **Valkyrien Skies** - Physics-based airship and vehicle framework
- **JEI** - Recipe viewing and item management
- **Embeddium & Oculus** - Performance optimizations and shaders support

## Getting Started

1. **Power Generation**: Start with a water wheel or windmill to generate kinetic energy
2. **Resource Automation**: Use mechanical drills and belts to automate mining
3. **Processing**: Build crushing wheels, millstones, and mixing stations
4. **Transportation**: Create conveyor belts and chute systems
5. **Vehicles**: Design your own airships and vehicles using Clockwork

## Tips for Inventors

- **Start Small**: Master the basics of rotational power before tackling complex builds
- **Plan Ahead**: Sketch your contraptions before building
- **Experiment**: The best inventions come from trial and error
- **Share**: Save your creations as schematics to share with others
