## Full description (paste below the line)

Progress from primitive tools and animal power to complex Create automation in a heavily integrated, quest-driven engineering modpack.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21 · Beta**

### The pitch

Inventors is not another pack that hands you a Create cogwheel on day one. Engineering has to be earned: you start with primitive survival and manual processing, graduate to animal-powered machinery, and only then unlock rotational automation. Every stage is stitched together with custom recipes and guided by quests.

### Progression

1. **Firstworks** — reworked early survival: primitive tools, animal materials, and tougher first days.
2. **The Quern** — your first real workstation. Grind flour, work dough, and process materials by hand.
3. **Animal power** — Horse Power CE turns your livestock into your first mechanical power grid.
4. **Create** — rotational automation at last, extended by Slice & Dice, Enchantment Industry, Power Grid, Fluid Logistics, Extra Gauges, and more.
5. **Industry** — Silent Gear tooling, Productive Metalworks, Building Gadgets, and Sophisticated Storage carry you into the late game.

### Key features

- Deliberately staged progression — nothing is free, everything connects
- Unified recipes and material flow across mods via custom KubeJS scripting
- FTB Quests chapter line that teaches every stage of the journey
- Season, worldgen, and biome overhauls (Serene Seasons, Terralith, Tectonic, Regions Unexplored)
- Farmer's Delight cooking ecosystem and animal materials from Naturalist wildlife
- Performance-tuned out of the box (Sodium, ModernFix, FerriteCore)

### Beta notice

Inventors is in active beta. Recipes, quests, and progression are still being balanced, and updates may change how your world plays. Keep world backups before updating.

### Multiplayer

Every release ships a dedicated server pack with pre-tuned configs and start scripts for Windows and Linux. Host it yourself or use any CurseForge-capable host.
